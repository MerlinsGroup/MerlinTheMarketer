Who We Are

About The
Company

Merlins Group was founded by two technical founders with deep roots in data science and data engineering. Our team has worked with Capgemini, Capita, IM Group, Aston University, University of Northampton, University of Birmingham, and Birmingham City Council — and we bring that enterprise-grade rigour to every marketing project: clean data pipelines, proper tracking architecture, and structured analytics.

One of our founders started in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) — understanding how humans interact with digital interfaces. That research is now published in Ergonomics, and it underpins everything we do: every page, every funnel, every conversion path is built on how people actually behave — not guesswork.

We apply machine learning, behavioural analytics, heatmap analysis, conversion modelling, and customer segmentation to find where your marketing is leaking money. We fix broken attribution, build proper data flows, and give you dashboards that tell you what is actually driving revenue — not vanity metrics.
