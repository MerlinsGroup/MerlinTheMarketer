Woodside Motor Services: Website, SEO & Local Marketing

How we built a high-converting website and local SEO strategy to help Woodside Motor Services dominate Birmingham search results.

18 November 2024 7 min read
Back to Blog

The Brief: A Garage That Needed More Than a Lick of Paint

Woodside Motor Services is a well-established independent garage in Birmingham, offering everything from MOTs and servicing to diagnostics and bodywork repairs. They had the skills, the reputation and the loyal customer base — but their digital presence told a different story. Their existing website was outdated, slow-loading and not mobile-friendly. They were invisible on Google for the very search terms their customers were typing in every single day.

When Woodside came to us, the brief was clear: build a website that actually converts visitors into bookings, and put them on the map — literally — for local search in Birmingham. They needed a partner who could handle everything from website development through to ongoing SEO, Google Ads and social media management.

The Website: Built to Convert

The first job was building Woodside a brand-new website from scratch. We did not just want it to look good — we wanted every page to serve a purpose. Every design decision was driven by one question: does this make it easier for someone to book?

What We Built

  • A fast, mobile-first website designed for customers searching on their phones — because that is how most people find a local garage
  • Clear service pages for MOTs, servicing, diagnostics, tyres, bodywork and more — each optimised for local search keywords
  • An integrated online booking system so customers can book an MOT or service in under 60 seconds, without needing to call
  • Trust signals throughout: customer reviews, accreditation badges, years of experience and a photo gallery of real work
  • A sticky call-to-action bar on mobile so the phone number and booking link are always one tap away
  • Schema markup for local business, services and reviews to give Google every reason to show Woodside in search results

The result is a website that loads fast, looks professional and — most importantly — turns visitors into paying customers. Within the first month of launch, online bookings accounted for over 35% of all new enquiries.

Local SEO: Owning Birmingham Search

A beautiful website means nothing if nobody can find it. For Woodside, the real game-changer was our local SEO strategy — specifically targeting the high-intent automotive keywords that Birmingham residents are searching for every day.

Google My Business Optimisation

We completely overhauled Woodside's Google My Business profile. This is the single most important asset for any local business, and most garages in Birmingham are barely using it. We optimised every field: business categories, service descriptions, opening hours, attributes and Q&A. We set up a system for regularly posting updates, offers and photos to keep the profile active and engaging.

We also implemented a review generation strategy. Happy customers were prompted to leave Google reviews after their service, and within three months Woodside's review count had more than doubled. The impact on local pack rankings was immediate.

On-Page and Technical SEO

Every page on the new website was built with SEO baked in from the start. We carried out extensive keyword research to identify the terms with the highest commercial intent in the Birmingham area:

  • "MOT near me Birmingham" and "MOT test Birmingham"
  • "car servicing Birmingham" and "full service Birmingham"
  • "car diagnostics Birmingham" and "engine management light Birmingham"
  • "brake repair Birmingham" and "clutch replacement Birmingham"
  • "garage near me" and "independent garage Birmingham"

Each service page was carefully structured with optimised title tags, meta descriptions, header hierarchy and internal linking. We built location-specific landing pages for the areas surrounding Woodside's location, ensuring they appeared in searches from across Birmingham and the wider West Midlands.

Content Strategy

We developed an ongoing content plan that positions Woodside as the go-to authority for automotive advice in Birmingham. Regular blog posts covering seasonal car maintenance tips, MOT preparation guides and common fault explanations help drive organic traffic and build topical authority. Every piece of content links back to relevant service pages, creating a strong internal link structure that reinforces their keyword targeting.

Google Ads: Immediate Visibility

While SEO is a long-term investment, Woodside needed results quickly. We launched a targeted Google Ads campaign alongside the SEO work to drive immediate traffic and bookings from day one.

Our approach was hyper-local and hyper-specific. We built out campaigns for each core service — MOTs, servicing, diagnostics and repairs — targeting a tight radius around Woodside's location. Ad copy was written to match the exact intent of each search, with clear calls to action and pricing where possible.

  • Search campaigns targeting high-intent keywords like "MOT booking Birmingham" and "car service near me"
  • Call-only ads for mobile users who want to phone directly
  • Location extensions showing Woodside's address, phone number and directions in every ad
  • Conversion tracking on both online bookings and phone calls so we could measure actual ROI
  • Negative keyword management to eliminate wasted spend on irrelevant searches

Within the first 90 days, the Google Ads campaigns delivered a cost-per-acquisition that was significantly below the industry average for automotive services, and online bookings from paid search were consistently growing month-on-month.

Social Media Management

Most garages do not think of social media as a priority — but for Woodside, it became a genuine driver of trust and repeat business. We took over their social media management across Facebook and Instagram, creating a consistent posting schedule that keeps their audience engaged.

Our content mix includes before-and-after repair photos, behind-the-scenes workshop footage, seasonal offers, customer testimonials and educational tips. We also run targeted local ads on Meta to reach Birmingham residents who may not yet know about Woodside but match the profile of their ideal customer.

The result is a social presence that feels human, trustworthy and active — reinforcing the brand every time a potential customer checks them out before booking.

Booking System Integration

One of the most impactful changes we made was integrating a seamless online booking system directly into the website. Before working with us, every booking came through phone calls — which meant missed calls during busy workshop hours, lost leads over weekends and no way to capture enquiries outside of opening hours.

The new system allows customers to select their service, choose a date and time, enter their vehicle details and confirm — all in under a minute. Bookings are automatically synced to Woodside's workshop calendar, and confirmation emails are sent to both the customer and the team. This one change alone reduced missed enquiries significantly and gave Woodside a 24/7 booking capability that their competitors simply do not have.

Automotive Expertise: From Independent Garages to National Brands

Woodside Motor Services is one of several automotive clients we work with. Our experience in this sector also includes GSF Car Parts — one of the UK's largest automotive parts distributors — where we handle content production, filming and marketing. This cross-sector experience gives us a deep understanding of what automotive customers are searching for, how they make decisions and what it takes to win their trust online.

Whether it is an independent garage in Birmingham or a national parts distributor, the fundamentals are the same: be visible where your customers are looking, make it easy to take action and build trust at every touchpoint. That is exactly what we delivered for Woodside.

The Results

Within six months of launching the new website and marketing strategy, Woodside Motor Services saw transformational growth across every metric that matters:

  • Organic traffic increased by over 340%, with the website now ranking on page one for dozens of high-intent local keywords
  • Woodside consistently appears in the Google Map Pack top 3 for key searches like "garage near me Birmingham" and "MOT Birmingham"
  • Monthly bookings more than doubled, with online bookings accounting for a growing share of total enquiries
  • Google Ads campaigns delivered a strong return on investment with cost-per-acquisition well below industry benchmarks
  • Social media following and engagement grew steadily, building long-term brand awareness in the local community
  • Google review count more than doubled, strengthening trust signals and local rankings

Woodside Motor Services is now one of the most visible garages in Birmingham — online and offline. The combination of a conversion-focused website, aggressive local SEO, targeted paid advertising and consistent social media has given them a digital presence that matches the quality of their workshop. They are not just keeping up with the competition — they are setting the standard.
